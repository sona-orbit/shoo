# Shoo! Stickers — hide windows on your Mac desktop with one click

Shoo is a hand-drawn sticker book for your Mac desktop. Stick a sticker on the desktop, tell it which apps or windows it looks after, and one click hides them all; click again and everything comes back exactly where it was. Handy before screen sharing or when you just want a clean desk. Available on the Mac App Store for macOS 14 or later.

[Website](https://sona-orbit.github.io/shoo/) · [Mac App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6826754561) · [Support](https://sona-orbit.github.io/shoo/support/) · [Privacy](https://sona-orbit.github.io/shoo/privacy/)

## Features

- **One click, clean desk**: click a sticker to hide its windows or apps, click again to bring them back.
- **One window or the whole app**: Safari, Chrome and Notes can hide a single window; other apps hide as a whole.
- **Hide desktop items too**: cover the files on your desktop with your own wallpaper while a sticker is active.
- **Stickers from your own photos**: drop in a photo and Shoo cuts it out right on your Mac; give each sticker its own set of windows.
- **Window marks**: a strip of washi tape on windows that belong to a sticker, tinted to match it.
- **Stay out of the way**: show Shoo in the Dock, the menu bar, or neither; open at login; put stickers on every desktop or just one.
- **Free to start**: the little red devil sticker comes with every feature. A one-time purchase unlocks more stickers, photo stickers and tabs. No subscription.
- **Private**: no account, no ads, no tracking. Shoo only hides and shows the windows you picked and never reads what is in them.
